More than meets the eye

There's far more to this fascinating Dales market town than meets the eye! Join a local guide for a relaxed stroll and uncover the surprising stories hidden in its streets, buildings and landscape.

Discover ginnels and snickets, old trades, local curiosities and the colourful characters who helped shape Settle — including a few secrets you might otherwise walk straight past.
